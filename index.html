Questions

Good to
know.

Is MRB a real store?
No. MRB is a concept brand created by María Luisa Ros Bolea for her portfolio. The products, prices and store are fictional; the design, copy and code are very real.
How does the VR demo work?
You book online, arrive five minutes early and a specialist fits the headset to you. Pick a world and live in it for 30, 45 or 60 minutes. It is free, and you do not have to buy anything.
How fast is delivery?
48 hours in mainland Spain, free over €500. Rest of the EU in 2–4 working days. You can also pick up in store the same day.
Can I return something?
You have 30 days to change your mind. We collect it from your home, check it in the lab and refund you within 72 hours.
Do you offer financing?
Yes: up to 12 instalments at 0% APR on orders over €300, approved at checkout.
